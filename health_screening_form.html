Before You Begin

This packet uses standardized screening tools that are used all over healthcare and coaching, and every one of my clients fills it out as a regular part of getting started, so you're in good company.

There's nothing here to pass or fail, I'm just getting a clear picture of where you are today so that I can support you the right way. Everything on it stays between us.

Section 1: Alcohol
AUDIT, the Alcohol Use Disorders Identification Test. Select one answer for each question.
1.How often do you have a drink containing alcohol?
2.How many standard drinks on a typical drinking day?
3.How often do you have six or more drinks on one occasion?
4.How often in the last year could you not stop drinking once you started?
5.How often in the last year did you fail to do what was normally expected due to drinking?
6.How often in the last year did you need a morning drink after a heavy session?
7.How often in the last year did you feel guilt or remorse after drinking?
8.How often in the last year were you unable to remember what happened due to drinking?
9.Have you or someone else been injured as a result of your drinking?
10.Has a relative, friend, or health worker been concerned about your drinking or suggested you cut down?
